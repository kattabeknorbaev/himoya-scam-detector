# Privacy Policy for Himoya Chrome Extension

**Last Updated:** September 2026

Himoya ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Himoya does **NOT** collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information or web browsing data. 

- **No Remote Servers**: Himoya does not connect to any external server or API.
- **No Analytics / Tracking**: We do not use Google Analytics, telemetry, cookies, or tracking scripts.
- **No Text Transmission**: All pattern matching, text analysis, and scam detection happen 100% locally inside your browser on your device.

## 2. Permissions Used
Himoya requests the minimum permissions necessary to function:
- **`storage`**: Used solely to save your local extension preferences (e.g. sensitivity level, whitelisted domains, and daily/total blocked scam counters) locally on your device via `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Content Scripts (`<all_urls>`)**: Required to detect suspicious Uzbek scam patterns across social media platforms, forums, and websites you browse. The content script analyzes text strictly within your browser's memory and does not record or transmit any page contents.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing
Because we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or disclose any user data to third parties.

## 4. Open Source Transparency
Himoya is open-source software. You can inspect the complete source code at any time on GitHub to verify our security and privacy claims.

## 5. Contact
If you have questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
