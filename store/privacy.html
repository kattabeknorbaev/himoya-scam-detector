🛡️ Himoya Privacy Policy

100% Client-Side • Zero Data Collection

Last updated: September 2026

Himoya ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a browser extension created to protect Uzbek-speaking internet users from digital financial scams and online phishing. This Privacy Policy details our strict commitment to privacy and data protection.

1. Absolute Zero Data Collection

Himoya does NOT collect, transmit, store, track, or share any personal information or web browsing history.

  • No External Server Communication: Himoya does not connect to any remote server or external API.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: There is no Google Analytics, tracking pixel, cookie, or user telemetry of any kind.
  • Local Processing: All scam detection patterns, string normalization, and content scanning execute 100% locally inside your browser memory on your personal machine.

2. Permissions Justification

  • Storage (chrome.storage.local): Used exclusively to persist user settings (such as sensitivity level and domain whitelist) and local threat counters directly on your device.
  • Content Scripts (<all_urls>): Necessary to evaluate text against suspicious fraud patterns in real-time across social media networks, forums, and blogs. Web page content never leaves your browser.

3. Open Source Transparency

Himoya is completely open source under the MIT License. Anyone can inspect and audit the source code on GitHub to verify our security guarantees.
